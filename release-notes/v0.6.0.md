### New

- FxmeGate: a noise gate and downward expander. Threshold with hysteresis, Range, Attack, Hold and Release, Gate or Expander mode (with a ratio), a high-pass and a low-pass on the detector with a Listen switch, optional look-ahead (0 to 5 ms, reported to the host as latency) and stereo-linked detection. The meter shows the detector's level against the threshold, which can be dragged, and the gain; a LED shows the state (open, hold, closed). Also as a Pd external, `fxmegate~`.

- FxmeDeEsser: a de-esser. A band-pass (frequency and Q) or high-pass
detector, Threshold, Range and a single Speed knob; Wideband mode turns the
whole signal down, Split band only the band (a dynamic bell or high shelf).
Relative detection compares the band with the whole signal, so the
threshold holds at any input level; Mid mode de-esses only the centre of a
stereo mix; Listen plays the band alone, to find the sibilance. A spectrum
shows the band (drag its handle to move it) and a meter the band's level
against the threshold, which can be dragged, and the reduction. Three
factory presets and a Pd external, `fxmedeesser~`.

- FxmeCrusher: a bit and sample-rate crusher, after an old sampler's signal
path. Drive into a converter that clips at full scale, Bits (1 to 24,
fractional) with a mid-tread, mid-rise or mu-law quantizer, Rate down to
200 Hz, Jitter on the length of each held sample to blur the aliasing
pattern, dither Noise that turns low bit depths into hiss, a Clean
anti-alias filter and a Smooth reconstruction filter, Mix and Output. A
waveform view shows the steps, input against output. Four factory presets
and a Pd external, `fxmecrusher~`.

- FxmeLimiter: a True Peak mode. It also limits the peaks between samples
(those a DAC rebuilds), read on a 4x oversampled copy of the signal as
ITU-R BS.1770 measures them, so the ceiling holds in dBTP. It adds 6
samples of delay. The test method and results are in doc/Limiter/.

- FxmeEqualizer: each band has a mode, Stereo (as before), Mid or Side, so
a band can filter only the mid or only the side of a stereo signal.

- FxmeTube: oversampling (Off, 2x, 4x). The whole stage runs at the higher
rate, so a hard drive no longer folds inharmonic aliases back into the
audio band. Off by default; when on, the small delay it adds is reported to
the host.

- FxmeConvolReverb: Low Cut and High Cut on the reverb (gentle 12 dB/octave
filters, off at their defaults), applied to the impulse response at no
running cost, or live on the reverb's output while tuning. The IR plot
shows the filtered response.

### Improved

- A new look across the collection: each effect has its own accent colour,
following its family (dynamics, modulation, tone, equalisation, delay,
reverb, spectral), console-style faders for the input, output and mix
levels, and reworked layouts (Compressor, Transient, Reverb, ConvolReverb
and others). Flanger and Phaser show their LFO's shape and phase, as the
Chorus already did.

- Frequency knobs (Equalizer, Phaser, StereoDelay, Oct, Reverb,
ConvolReverb) turn on a logarithmic scale, so the low and mid range is no
longer squeezed into the first tenth of the travel. Sessions and automation
are unchanged.

- Every effect is now protected against bad input: NaN or infinite samples
are removed on entry, and the effects with feedback (StereoDelay, Flanger,
Phaser, Chorus) keep oversized samples out of their loops, so one bad
sample from upstream can no longer silence an effect or make it output
NaN for good.

### For developers

- FxmeNet: every effect can be driven remotely through the FxmeTools
network bridge, with its meters and displays (gain reduction, LFO phase)
sent as telemetry.

- Accent colours are centralised in Source/Common/Theme.h, and shared
control set-ups (faders, gain faders) in Source/Common/Controls.h.
