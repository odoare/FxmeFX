### New

- FxmeCab: an IR of your own on each side. A Load... button beside each IR
chooser takes a WAV, AIFF or FLAC file, which then plays in that slot (the
chooser's last entry, "External file", shows its name; picking the entry with
nothing loaded opens the file chooser). The file's audio is stored in the
session and in presets, so no file is needed afterwards, and it is normalised
to the same loudness as the built-in cabinets. The left and right sides each
have their own. Sessions and presets made before are unchanged.

- FxmeStereoDelay: a Ping-pong switch. The repeats cross from one side to the
other (the left delay feeds the right, the right feeds the left), so the
echoes bounce. Each side's Fdbk sets what it sends across; Cross is then what
stays on the same side. Moving the switch crossfades in 30 ms, with no click.
Off by default, so existing sessions and presets sound as before.
`fxmestereodelay~` gains an inlet (14), after the existing ones.

### Improved

- FxmeTube: the window opens a little larger (500 x 350 instead of 400 x 300),
more in width than in height, so the knobs have a size closer to the other
effects' without a tall tube picture and Output fader. Resizing is unchanged.

- FxmeLimiter and FxmeTransient: a lower window (220 pixels under the top
bar instead of 300), with a shorter gain-reduction meter; the window can
also be made lower than before.

- README: the Mid / Side mode of FxmeEqualizer's bands is described (what St,
M and S do, how the button and the graph show them), and FxmeCab's own IRs.
